Rete Wall – Who coined the term and when?

The “Rete Wall” is a commonly used industry term, referring to potential performance limits of a standard Rete algorithm implementaiton.

I’ve been googling and I cannot see this term used in google scholar at all, and the earliest reference i could find to this term was a JRules white paper, in 2006.

So I’m wondering who first coined this term and when?  Leave comments if you have the answer, and i’ll update this blog.
