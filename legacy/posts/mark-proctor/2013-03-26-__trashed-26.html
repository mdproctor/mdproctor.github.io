Daniel Miranker will be keynoting at this year’s IntelliFest. Daniel is a professor of Computer Sciences at the University of Texas and the creator of the TREAT and LEAPS pattern matching algorithms. Both algorithms address the eager nature of the Rete algorithm. LEAPS itself is big influence on the new Drools algorithm we are close to completion on.
