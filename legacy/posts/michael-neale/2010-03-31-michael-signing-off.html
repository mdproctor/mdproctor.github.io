Michael – signing off

by Michael Neale - March 31, 2010
General Article
Well the time has come for me (Michael) to say goodbye – to Red Hat. I must turn into the force, just as yoda did. I will of course remain a community member – using and developing drools.
It has been a blast working on this stuff fulltime for the last few years – and to see the community grow so much. There is now so much going on no one person can really keep up in detail – didn’t used to be like that (not really that long ago either !) – things are in brilliant shape.
So thanks everyone for your support and advice over the years. Hopefully I will have more to blog about here soon !
For those interested, my personal blog (which I will put more details up on when I can) is here.
So long, and thanks for all the fish ! And see you around the mailing lists etc !
